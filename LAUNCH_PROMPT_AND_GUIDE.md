## 🛠️ PART 2: 3-MINUTE ZERO-CODE LAUNCH GUIDE (VERCEL / NETLIFY)

Aapko kisi bhi AI ki zaroorat nahi padegi agar aap ye 5 simple steps follow karein:

### Step 1: Create a GitHub Account & Repository (2 Minutes)
1. Go to [github.com](https://github.com) (free sign up).
2. Click **New Repository** ➔ Name it `MyStudyAI` ➔ Select **Public** ➔ Click **Create Repository**.
3. Click **"uploading an existing file"** and drag all files from your `MyStudyAI` folder:
   - `StudyOS.html`
   - `index.html`
   - `ambient-audio.js`
   - `aura-companion.js`
   - `error-monitor.js`
   - `vercel.json`
   - `manifest.json`
   - `api/quiz.js`
4. Click **Commit changes**.

### Step 2: Deploy Free on Vercel (1 Minute)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) ➔ Sign in with your **GitHub**.
2. Click **"Add New Project"** ➔ Select your `MyStudyAI` repository.
3. Under **Environment Variables**, add:
   - Key: `GEMINI_API_KEY`
   - Value: `your_gemini_api_key_here`
4. Click **"Deploy"** 🚀!

**Result**: 30 seconds ke andar aapka app live ho jayega aur aapko ek link mil jayega:
👉 `https://mystudyai.vercel.app` (ya jo bhi naam aap chahein).

## 🌐 PART 3: CONNECTING CUSTOM DOMAIN (e.g. auraspace.in)
1. Buy domain from GoDaddy / Namecheap / Hostinger (costs ₹200–₹500/year).
2. In Vercel Dashboard ➔ Go to **Settings** ➔ **Domains** ➔ Enter your domain.
3. Follow the 2 DNS records shown by Vercel. Done!

---

## 💰 PART 4: MONETIZATION & ADSENSE SUBMISSION
1. Once your custom domain is live on Vercel with HTTPS, go to [google.com/adsense](https://www.google.com/adsense).
2. Submit your website URL.
3. Google AdSense will check for **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** (which are already integrated inside your AuraSpace Settings).
4. Approval usually takes 2–5 days.
